Welcome to AcreetionOS XFCE

AcreetionOS XFCE is a fast, stable, and low-resource spinoff featuring the XFCE desktop environment. XFCE embodies the traditional UNIX philosophy of modularity and reusability, providing a lightweight yet fully-featured desktop that stays out of your way.

Why XFCE?

XFCE is the gold standard for lightweight Linux desktops. It's incredibly fast, uses very little RAM, and is famous for its rock-solid stability. If you're running on older hardware, or if you just want your computer to feel instantaneous, XFCE is the perfect choice. It's highly customizable through its panel and plugin system, allowing you to create the exact workflow you want.

Rock-Solid XLibre Foundation

While other desktops are rushing to Wayland, XFCE remains the king of X11. Running on our modernized XLibre foundation, AcreetionOS XFCE offers unparalleled compatibility with all graphics drivers and legacy applications. No flickering, no screen-sharing issues—just a desktop that works.

Lightweight Tools

The AcreetionOS XFCE Edition ships with the efficient XFCE core components:

  • File Manager: Thunar — Fast, reliable, and easy to use.
  • Terminal: XFCE Terminal — Lightweight with essential features.
  • Text Editor: Mousepad — Simple and quick for all text tasks.
  • Image Viewer: Ristretto — Optimized for speed and low memory usage.
  • Settings: XFCE Settings Manager — Complete control over every detail.
  • Audio: Pipewire — Modern audio performance on a classic desktop.
